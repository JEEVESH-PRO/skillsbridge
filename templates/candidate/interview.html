01
Coding & Algorithmic Round

02
System Architecture Round

Architect scalable microservices, database schemas, caching strategy (Redis), load balancing, and message queues (Kafka/RabbitMQ).

03
Behavioral STAR Framework

Prepare Situation, Task, Action, Result stories emphasizing leadership, conflict resolution, technical ownership, and learning from failure.
